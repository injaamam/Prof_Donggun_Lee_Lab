Graduate Students The Intelligent Controls Lab is currently recruiting 2–3 Ph.D. students. Prospective students should apply to the NC State Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (MAE) graduate program through the official admissions process:

MAE Graduate Admissions

When completing your application, please indicate Prof. Donggun Lee as a faculty member you are interested in working with. You are also encouraged to contact Prof. Lee by email ( donggun_lee@ncsu.edu ). Please include the following information: Email subject: [PhD Application] A current CV/resume A brief description of your research interests A brief summary of your background and technical strengths Relevant coursework in areas such as control, robotics, optimization, mathematics, or programming

Postdoctoral Positions The Intelligent Controls Lab welcomes inquiries from prospective postdoctoral researchers with interests aligned with our research areas. Interested candidates should contact Prof. Donggun Lee directly by email and include the following information: Email subject: [Postdoc Application] A current CV A brief description of your research interests and proposed research direction A summary of your relevant research experience and expertise Links to 2–3 representative publications or your Google Scholar profile Your expected availability/start date
